Build. Connect. Grow. Together.

Commun is not another generic social network. It is the dedicated collaboration and professional acceleration engine designed specifically for software engineers, technical founders, open-source maintainers, and developer organizations.

The Core Engine: The Commun Product Loop

Every feature in Commun is engineered to propel you through this virtuous cycle:

01. DISCOVER

Find talented engineers, active repositories, and emerging technologies.

02. CONNECT

Build your verified developer network and collaborate with like-minded peers.

03. DISCUSS

Engage in high-signal RFCs, architecture debates, and accepted technical answers.

04. COLLABORATE

Publish collaboration requests and match with skilled teammates.

05. BUILD

Turn ideas into code, manage sprints, track issues, and ship releases.

06. SHARE

Broadcast build updates, open-source libraries, and architectural lessons.

07. OPPORTUNITIES

Get discovered for jobs, grants, bounties, and hackathon prizes.

08. GROW

Evolve from junior builder to respected community leader and architect.
