Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Commun subscriptions and workspaces.

Can individual developers always use Commun for free? +
Yes! Individual profiles, participating in open-source projects, asking/answering questions, and discovering opportunities are 100% free forever for engineers.
How does multi-tenancy work for colleges and companies? +
Organizations receive an isolated tenant workspace with customized branding, member seat allocation, pod structuring, role permissions (Admin, Manager, Member, Guest), and private repository collaboration.
Can I switch between monthly and annual plans at any time? +
Yes. Upgrades are prorated immediately, and downgrades apply at the close of your current billing cycle without penalties.
